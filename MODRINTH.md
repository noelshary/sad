# 🎨 HitColor – every hit in YOUR color

Tired of the boring red flash? **HitColor** lets you pick how hits look.

### What it does
Hit a mob or player and it lights up in the color **you** choose – subtle and see-through or loud and neon. Press **H** (or open it through Mod Menu), pick a color, drag the strength slider, done.

### Features
- 🎨 40 handpicked colors + any HEX color
- 🔆 Strength slider: transparent ⇄ fully solid
- ✨ Animations: Pulse, Rainbow, Shimmer, Strobe
- 💥 Colored hit particles
- 🔔 Optional hit sounds with volume control
- 👀 Live preview right in the menu

### Good to know
- Client-side only, works on every server
- Needs Fabric API, Mod Menu is optional
- Minecraft 1.21.11
